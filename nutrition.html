Before your first session

Please complete these six forms before your first nutrition session. Work through them in order. They only take a few minutes each, so we can spend our first session on your goals and your plan rather than on paperwork.

Do you already train with us?

You've already filled in your client details and privacy notice on your training onboarding, so you only need the forms below.

    Client details

    Nutrition health questionnaire

    Allergies and intolerances, medical conditions, medication, pregnancy or breastfeeding, and any history of disordered eating. It helps me check that nutrition coaching is right for you, or whether to involve your GP or a dietitian first.

    Open form
  3. 03

    Lifestyle and goals

    Your goal, height and current weight, a typical day of eating, food likes and dislikes, who you cook for, cooking time and budget, and your work pattern and activity, so your plan fits the way you actually live.

    Open form
  4. 04

    Nutrition terms and conditions

    What's included, weekly check-ins and response times, billing every 4 weeks and how to cancel, and that nutrition coaching isn't medical advice.

    Open form
  5. 05

    Privacy notice

    How your personal, health and nutrition information is collected, stored and used, who can see it, and your rights over it.

    Open form
